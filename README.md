# Ploomi Coffee by Bermito

B2B coffee website, generated as static multi-page HTML with shared CSS and JavaScript. No online checkout or visitor database. Forms prepare an enquiry for the visitor to review and send in WhatsApp or their own email app; the UI explicitly explains this.

## Update

Edit `build.py` for content/page structure; `dist/styles.css` and `dist/app.js` for styling and behaviour. Run `python3 build.py`. The Sites manifest points to `dist`.

The three approved logo compositions are recorded in `BRAND.md`. The shared header uses the balanced horizontal SVG; footer, About and packs use the stacked SVG. Favicons use the original two-O symbol, with ICO and Apple touch support. Generated illustration originals are retained in `source-assets`, with lightweight WebP versions deployed. `ASSET-SOURCES.md` records Bermito asset provenance.

## Confirmed commercial scope

- Wholesale and custom blends, brewing/menu support, training and equipment/café guidance.
- No private labelling, no checkout, no minimum wholesale quantity.
- Pricing quoted after requirements. Payment terms discussed directly.
- Free wholesale delivery across Kerala.
- Paid samples with separate delivery charges, costs/availability confirmed before payment.
- Tiramisu, House and Christmas blends are explicitly illustrative concepts; no invented product origin, sensory claims, prices or availability.
- Bermito's public email and telephone are used. Telephone was verified on the official contact page; WhatsApp account availability was not independently confirmed.
