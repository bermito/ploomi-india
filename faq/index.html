GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.
Straight answers.

Is there a minimum wholesale order?+

No. There is no minimum order quantity for wholesale coffee. Tell us what you need and we’ll prepare a quotation.

Where do you deliver?+

We supply across Kerala and India. Wholesale delivery within Kerala is free; delivery outside Kerala is confirmed in your quotation. Paid samples have separate delivery charges, confirmed before payment.

How much does the coffee cost?+

Wholesale pricing is quoted after we understand your coffee preferences, quantity and business needs. Payment terms are discussed directly.

Can I try samples first?+

Yes. Samples are paid, and delivery charges apply. You can choose your preferred direction or ask for recommendations. We’ll confirm availability, sample cost and delivery before you pay.

Are the blends on this website available now?+

Tiramisu Blend, House Blend and Christmas Blend are illustrative concepts for our developing range. Their packaging, profiles and availability are not final. Get in touch to discuss current sampling options.

Can you make a blend for my café?+

We welcome custom-blend enquiries. Tell us about your menu, brewing setup and flavour preferences so we can discuss what is possible.

Can I order and pay through the website?+

For now, all orders begin with an enquiry by WhatsApp or email. We confirm the coffee, quotation, delivery and payment terms directly.

Do you offer private labelling?+

No. Private labelling is not part of our current offering. We can discuss Ploomi wholesale supply and custom blends for your café.
